============================================================
ALBUM:  Do Not Push the Horses ("Та нє гані канєй")
ARTIST: Dj Vasyl-hucul
TRACK:  07 — Wifi on the Polonyna
============================================================

[METADATA]
- Genre / Style : Synth-Pop / Ethno-Disco
- Tempo (BPM)   : 120 BPM
- Mood / Vibe   : Кумедний, сучасний, легка сатира
- Language      : English + Ukrainian / Hutsul / Surzhyk inserts

[CONCEPT]
Історія про айтішника чи діджея, який поїхав у гори на детокс від цивілізації, але все одно шукає хоча б одну паличку інтернету, щоб закинути новий трек і сторіз.

[KEY UKRAINIAN / SURZHYK PHRASES]
- Нема зв'язку
- Ловить на горбі
- Вівці і роутер
- Та вимкни той телефон

[STRUCTURE]
Intro -> Verse 1 -> Pre-Chorus -> Chorus -> Verse 2 -> Bridge -> Final Chorus -> Outro

[LYRICS]
[Intro]
(Retro 80s synth-pop arpeggio + sound of sheep bells + phone "no signal" beep)
(Spoken, slightly dramatic)
"Offline mode activated..."
Нема зв'язку!

[Verse 1]
Packed my backpack, said "Goodbye to the screen!
I'm going digital detox, living pure and green!"
Two hours in, and my thumb starts to twitch
Need to post a sunset pic, scratch that upload itch
Climbing on a boulder, standing on one toe
Holding up my smartphone like a disco radio
Shepherd walks by, shakes his head and grins:
"Ловить на горбі, right where the cliff begins!"

[Pre-Chorus]
One bar... no bars... wait, I saw 4G!
Don't move a muscle, don't even breathe!
Loading... spinning... ninety-nine percent...
And then a cloud came by and my signal went!

[Chorus]
Looking for the Wi-Fi on the polonyna high!
Standing on a rock with my phone in the sky!
Нема зв'язку — guess I'll dance with the sheep!
My battery's at five and my notifications sleep!
Wi-Fi on the polonyna, where did you go?
Вівці і роутер — welcome to the show!

[Verse 2]
Got a brand-new banger ready to drop
Dj Vasyl-hucul climbing straight to the top
Thought I'd push to cloud from a wooden hut
Instead I'm syncing rhythms with a mountain strut
Local grandma laughing from the garden gate:
"Та вимкни той телефон, it can wait!
Look at the mountains, breathe the pine-tree air
The best connection isn't floating up there!"

[Bridge]
(Synth drops to a dreamy pad, sheep bell keeps the beat)
Airplane mode: ON.
Stress level: GONE.
Та вимкни той телефон...
Wait — maybe the sheep were right all along.
No loading circle in the real world!

[Final Chorus]
No more Wi-Fi on the polonyna high!
Put the phone in my pocket, look up at the sky!
Нема зв'язку — and I'm dancing with the sheep!
Best kind of signal is the rhythm that we keep!
No Wi-Fi on the polonyna, and I feel so free!
Ловить на горбі — just the mountains and me!

[Outro]
(Playful synth melody fades out, one last cheerful sheep "Baaa!")
Та вимкни той телефон.
О, тепер файно.
